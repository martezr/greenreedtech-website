This Privacy Policy explains how Green Reed Technology ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit [greenreedtech.com](https://greenreedtech.com) (the "Site").

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.** This is a personal blog and portfolio site. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no ad networks, and no analytics services that track you across the web.

## What We Collect

### Cookies

The Site uses a small number of cookies strictly necessary for the Site to function correctly:

- **Theme preference cookie** — remembers whether you have selected light or dark mode.
- **Cookie consent cookie** — records that you have acknowledged this policy so the consent banner is not shown again.

These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information, are never shared with third parties, and are stored only in your browser.

### Server Logs

Our hosting provider may automatically record standard server log data when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of your request. This data is retained by the hosting provider for operational and security purposes and is not accessible to or used by us for tracking or analytics.

## What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, or store:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Payment or financial information
- Location data
- Behavioral or browsing data across other sites
- Any other personally identifiable information

## Third-Party Services

### Embedded Content

Blog posts may include images or other content hosted on third-party services (such as Amazon S3). When you load a page containing such content, your browser may make a request directly to those services, which may log your IP address per their own privacy policies.

### External Links

The Site contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

## No Sharing of Personal Information

**We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any personal information with third parties.** Because we do not collect personal information in the first place, there is nothing to share.

## Your Cookie Choices

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Declining or deleting cookies may affect the Site's ability to remember your theme preference, but will not prevent you from accessing any content.

## Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
